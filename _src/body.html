How a re-roof goes on

The first plays a slope being tiled from bare battens up. Drag the handle on the second to move between the roof mid job and the finished covering.

Job 01

Stripped, felted, battened and re-tiled

The old covering comes off, the roof is felted with a breathable membrane and battened at the right gauge, then the tiles go on course by course from the eaves up. The gauge staying constant all the way to the ridge is what keeps the courses straight and stops the slope drifting off line halfway up.

Job 02

Hips, valleys and ridges given the time

Hips and valleys are where a roof lets go first, so that is where the hours go. Tiles are cut in tight along the hip, the ridge is bedded and pointed, and the slope is left running true from the eaves to the apex rather than drifting off line halfway up.

Why choose us

The bit you cannot see is the bit that matters

Anyone can lay a tile. Whether the roof is still dry in five winters comes down to the membrane, the battens and the lead underneath it.

Sound underneath, not just on top

New breathable membrane and treated battens on every re-roof. Tiles dropped back onto tired felt is a job you end up paying for twice.

Leadwork done, not patched

Chimneys, valleys and abutments get proper lead, dressed and fixed, rather than a smear of mastic that lasts one winter.

One team, start to finish

The person who quotes the roof is on the roof. Nothing gets handed off to a subcontractor you have never met.
